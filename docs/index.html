Sacred Classical Philosophy

The Authentic Eight Limbs of Ashtanga Yoga

Codified by Sage Patañjali in the classical Yoga Sutras, Ashtanga (literally ashta = eight, anga = limbs) is a holistic, progressive path of spiritual awakening, ethical living, and mental stillness.

यमनियमासनप्राणायामप्रत्याहारधारणाध्यानसमाधयोऽष्टावङ्गानि ॥ २.२९ ॥
yama-niyama-āsana-prāṇāyāma-pratyāhāra-dhāraṇā-dhyāna-samādhayo ’ṣṭāv aṅgāni
"The eight limbs of yoga are: moral restraints (Yama), internal observances (Niyama), physical postures (Asana), breath control (Pranayama), sensory withdrawal (Pratyahara), concentration (Dharana), meditation (Dhyana), and meditative absorption (Samadhi)."
Patañjali’s Yoga Sutras • Sadhana Pada 2.29

In traditional wisdom, the eight limbs do not form a mere checklist of physical exercises, but an integrated living tree. The practice begins with universal ethics and self-discipline (Bahiranga, outer sadhana) and culminates in meditative realization and transcendent bliss (Antaranga, inner sadhana).

The physical posture sequence (Asana, the 3rd limb) and synchronized breath (Pranayama, the 4th limb) act as the vital practical doorway. By building internal heat (tapas), detoxifying the physical organs and energy pathways (nadis), and anchoring awareness through the Tristana method (Breath, Bandhas, and Drishti), the turbulent mind is brought to natural stillness: “yogaś citta-vṛtti-nirodhaḥ”.

Limb 01 ⚖️
यम

Yama

Universal Ethical Disciplines

The foundational moral guidelines governing our relationship with society, all living creatures, and the world. Yamas establish the inner tranquility and clear conscience essential for contemplative practice.

The 5 Restraints: • Ahimsa (Non-violence & compassion)
• Satya (Truthfulness & integrity)
• Asteya (Non-stealing)
• Brahmacharya (Energy moderation)
• Aparigraha (Non-possessiveness & simplicity)
Limb 02 🌿
नियम

Niyama

Personal Self-Purification

Internal observances and self-disciplines that purify personal habits, emotional balance, and devotion. They nurture the fertile inner soil required for spiritual growth.

The 5 Observances: • Saucha (Purity of body, diet & mind)
• Santosha (Contentment with what is)
• Tapas (Disciplined spiritual heat)
• Svadhyaya (Self-study & scriptures)
• Ishvara Pranidhana (Surrender to higher wisdom)
Limb 03 🧘
आसन

Asana

Posture & Steady Seat

Defined in Yoga Sutra 2.46 as “Sthira Sukham Asanam” — posture must be both steady and joyful. In Ashtanga Yoga, progressive physical series (Yoga Chikitsa) detoxify tissues, strengthen muscles, and align the spine so the body can sit without restlessness.

Ashtanga Embodiment: Dynamic breath-synchronized vinyasas cleanse internal organs, build endurance, balance nervous systems, and cultivate lightness (laghu).
Limb 04 💨
प्राणायाम

Pranayama

Breath & Life Force Expansion

Conscious mastery and expansion of prana (vital life energy). Regulating inhalation (puraka), exhalation (rechaka), and breath retention (kumbhaka) harmonizes mental fluctuations and vital organs.

Ujjayi Breathing: In Ashtanga, deep diaphragmatic Ujjayi (Victorious Ocean Breath) maintains steady rhythmic pacing (Sama Vritti) and stokes the internal fire (agni).
Limb 05 👁️
प्रत्याहार

Pratyahara

Sense Withdrawal & Inward Turning

Retracting the outward-seeking senses from external stimuli, just as a tortoise withdraws its limbs into its shell. The mind ceases reacting to external sights and sounds and redirects its gaze inward.

Ashtanga Embodiment: Practiced continuously on the mat by anchoring the eyes to specific gaze points (Drishti) and tuning to the internal sound of the ocean breath.
Limb 06 🎯
धारणा

Dharana

One-Pointed Concentration

Binding the attention of the mind to a single point, center, or object without wavering (desha-bandhas cittasya dharana). It trains consciousness into unwavering stability.

Ashtanga Embodiment: Maintained by counting exact Sanskrit vinyasa numbers (Ekam, Dve, Trīṇi...), breath holds, and internal locks (Bandhas).
Limb 07 🪷
ध्यान

Dhyana

Meditation & Continuous Awareness

The continuous, unbroken stream of contemplative awareness toward the object of meditation, uninterrupted like oil flowing from one vessel to another.

Ashtanga Embodiment: When movement, breath, and gaze unite seamlessly, the entire practice transforms into an effortless, ecstatic moving meditation.
Limb 08 ☀️
समाधि

Samadhi

Absorption & Spiritual Union

The ultimate culmination of yoga: pure superconscious absorption. The illusory separation between the practitioner, the act of meditation, and the Divine dissolves into supreme peace, clarity, and oneness.

The State of Yoga: Resting effortlessly in the pristine stillness of the true Self (Purusha), liberated from mental restlessness and suffering.
The Ashtanga Bridge

The Tristana Method: Uniting Body, Breath & Mind

How does physical movement become spiritual transformation? In Ashtanga Yoga, the Tristana method simultaneously binds three sacred focal points throughout every posture, linking the outer limbs directly to inner meditation:

🌊

1. Ujjayi Pranayama (Breath)

Smooth, diaphragmatic breathing with gentle throat constriction creates a resonant ocean sound. Equalizes inhalation and exhalation, detoxifies cells, and anchors present-moment awareness.

🔒

2. Bandhas (Energy Locks)

Internal energetic seals—Mula Bandha (pelvic root lock) and Uddiyana Bandha (lower abdominal lock)—channel prana upward through the spine, producing physical lightness and core stability.

🎯

3. Drishti (Focal Gaze)

Designated gazing points (nose tip, third eye, navel, hands, toes, sky) steady the optic nerve. By directing the eyes, the mind stops wandering and turns effortlessly inward (Pratyahara).
